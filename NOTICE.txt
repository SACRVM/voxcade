Voxcade: what its licence covers
================================

The licence in LICENSE.txt (the zlib licence, as LÖVE has it) covers the Voxcade
program, the games that come with it (More Blood, Voxkeep, Sokovox,
Pulsar, BunnyFarm: their code, figures, levels, sounds and songs) and
the templates new games begin from. In short: use it for free, make
games with it, give them away or sell them, and pass the program on
with them. A game you make with Voxcade is yours alone.

Not ours to license, and passed on under their own terms:

- Sokovox's puzzles are Microban by David W. Skinner, free to pass on
  provided they remain properly credited (games/sokovox/levels/).
- Several songs are composed from chord progressions of free-midi-chords
  by Ludovic Drolez (MIT License).
- The big letters of the games' titles are drawn from Fira Sans
  Compressed Heavy (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
- The libraries built into the program: THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt.

The online services (the game codes, the list of games, the relay) are
offered as they are, without a promise that they stay; they are no part
of this licence.
